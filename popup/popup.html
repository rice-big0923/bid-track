Bid Track is available (you have ).

  1. Download it and unzip it over your Bid Track folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and click Reload on Bid Track.

Sign in with your Engineers login and pick the profile this Chrome profile records for. To track another profile, sign in on another Chrome profile.

Allow Bid Track to record the full location of files you attach on job pages (for example C:\Users\me\Documents\Resumes\resume.pdf) so your manager can check the right resume was sent? You can change this later in Settings.
